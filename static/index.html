Step by Step

How Buying Property in Israel Works

From your first inquiry to receiving the keys.

Property Search

We identify properties matching your criteria. Virtual tours available for overseas clients.

Remote-friendly

Lawyer & Power of Attorney

A Power of Attorney allows your lawyer to sign on your behalf. Can be notarized at the Israeli consulate in your country.

Required

Mortgage Pre-Approval

Non-residents can borrow up to 50% of the property value. A mortgage advisor will compare bank offers on your behalf.

Up to 50% LTV

Due Diligence & Contract

Your lawyer checks title, liens, and permits. The purchase contract is signed. A deposit of 10-15% is paid at signing.

10-15% deposit

Purchase Tax (Mas Rechisha)

Foreign residents pay Purchase Tax starting at 8% on the first ₪6.05M and 10% above. Your lawyer files and pays this tax.

8-10% for non-residents

Closing & Registration

Balance is paid, keys are handed over, and your lawyer registers the property in the Land Registry (Tabu). Typically 3-6 months total.

3-6 months total
